PLATFORM: AI-Powered Smart EV Charging Network & Grid Load Management System

================================================================================
1. PROJECT ABSTRACT
================================================================================

The rapid adoption of Electric Vehicles (EVs) across India presents dual 
challenges: EV drivers face unpredictability regarding charging slot 
availability and long queue times, while electrical utility distribution 
companies (DISCOMs) suffer from local transformer overloads caused by uncoordinated 
high-power charging demand.

BOSS (Bharat EV Optimization & Smart Charging System) is an end-to-end, 
intelligent, real-time EV charging network and grid management platform designed 
to bridge the gap between EV Drivers, Station Admins, and DISCOM Utilities.

The platform integrates Machine Learning (ML) regressors, an automated 80% EV 
Station Load Safety Rule, OpenRouter Large Language Model (LLM) grid intelligence, 
and 1-Hour Slot Allocation with 6-digit Smart Energy Meter OTP verification. 

By combining interactive map discovery, real-time port-level power telemetry, 
predictive battery diagnostics, and dynamic peak shaving incentives, BOSS 
eliminates driver range anxiety, maximizes station utilization, and protects 
DISCOM grid infrastructure from voltage fluctuations and transformer failures.


================================================================================
2. SYSTEM WORKING & ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================

The BOSS platform operates through three synchronized user roles connected to a 
unified Supabase PostgreSQL database and an OpenRouter AI Copilot inference engine.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. EV DRIVER PORTAL (USER DASHBOARD)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Interactive Station Discovery & Maps:
   - EV drivers explore charging stations across top Indian states (Maharashtra, 
     Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Delhi, Kerala, Gujarat, UP, etc.) and specific 
     districts using interactive Leaflet map visuals.
   - Drivers can filter by station operator, port freeness, distance, and 
     connector types (CCS2, Type 2, CHAdeMO).

2. AI Recommendation & Free Slot Finder:
   - The AI engine evaluates distance (Haversine formula), queue wait times, 
     current battery level, price/kWh, and transformer load percentage to 
     compute a 0–100% Explainable AI Match Score for every station.

3. 1-Hour Slot Reservation & Smart Meter OTP Code:
   - Drivers lock 1-hour prebooking slots for TODAY ONLY.
   - Upon reservation, the system generates an encrypted 6-digit numeric/letter 
     Smart Energy Meter OTP verification code expiring at the end of the slot.

4. 80% EV Station Load Rule (Automated Safety Redirection):
   - To prevent transformer overload, if a selected station's load reaches or 
     exceeds 80% of its transformer capacity (kVA), the AI engine automatically 
     redirects the driver to a nearby alternative station within the district.

5. Machine Output Voltage & Power Dispensing Calculation:
   - Unedited Machine Output DC Voltage (V) and Power Output (kW) are dynamically 
     computed using Machine Learning linear regressors based on the vehicle's 
     current battery percentage and selected charge current (16A, 32A, 64A, 150A).

6. Emergency Mode & Mobile Battery Rescue Dispatch:
   - One-touch emergency priority allocation for emergency EV vehicles.
   - On-demand Mobile Battery Rescue Truck dispatching equipped with portable 
     power packs (20 kWh, 30 kWh, 50 kWh) featuring live ETA tracking.

7. Dedicated Full-Page Intelligence Modules:
   - Vehicle Details: Large vehicle visual showcase, Indian EV green number plate, 
     battery kWh capacity, real-world range, degradation rate (1.1%/yr), battery 
     temperature (28.5°C), and regenerative braking efficiency (94.2%).
   - Energy Analytics: Weekly consumption bar charts, 24-hour charging load area 
     charts, tariff rate breakdowns, and DISCOM green energy savings insights.
   - Station Map Intelligence: Full-height interactive Leaflet map, district 
     filters, AI top station rankings, and route optimization.
   - Charging History & Ledger: Filterable session logs, smart meter verification 
     receipts, and carbon credit credit statements.
   - Slot Booking & Power Dispatch: 24 1-hour slot grid selector, charge current 
     selectors, battery level sliders, and instant slot receipt generation.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
B. STATION ADMIN DASHBOARD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Transformer Load Management:
   - Live monitoring of transformer capacity limits (kVA) and active load draw.
2. Smart Meter Verification Terminal:
   - Station operators enter user 6-digit OTP booking codes to verify eligibility 
     and activate DC fast power dispensing.
3. Port-Level Charger Control:
   - Real-time monitoring of charger statuses ('available', 'occupied', 'fault') 
     and per-port power distribution across ports A, B, C, D.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
C. DISCOM ELECTRICAL UTILITY DASHBOARD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Grid Demand & Substation Telemetry:
   - High-level overview of district power demand, peak grid hours, and transformer 
     stress points.
2. Dynamic Peak Shaving Incentives:
   - Pushes tariff discounts (e.g., ₹1.5/kWh off-peak rebate) to encourage EV 
     charging during low-demand hours (11:00 PM – 06:00 AM).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D. OPENROUTER AI COPILOT & GRID INTELLIGENCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Natural Language Conversational Assistant:
   - Integrated floating AI Copilot widget powered by LLMs (Google Gemini 2.5 Flash, 
     DeepSeek R1, Llama 3.3).
2. Live Context Awareness:
   - Answers questions regarding nearest free slots, grid load warnings, optimal 
     charging times, and station transformer health.


================================================================================
3. STEP-BY-STEP WORKING FLOW
================================================================================

[Step 1: Discovery]
Driver opens the BOSS User Dashboard, views interactive India state maps, and 
identifies nearby stations.

[Step 2: AI Recommendation]
AI engine ranks stations using distance, price, wait time, and transformer load.

[Step 3: Slot Booking & Safety Check]
Driver selects a 1-hour slot for TODAY and clicks "Confirm Booking". The system 
checks the 80% EV Station Load Rule:
- If Load < 80%: Slot is reserved; 6-digit OTP code is generated.
- If Load >= 80%: Driver is redirected to an optimal nearby station in the district.

[Step 4: ML Telemetry Computation]
The ML engine computes exact Output Voltage (e.g., 400V DC) and Power (e.g., 50kW) 
based on the vehicle's battery percentage and requested current.

[Step 5: Smart Meter Verification]
Driver arrives at the station and inputs the 6-digit OTP code into the Admin/Meter 
terminal. The system verifies the code, activates the charger port, and dispenses 
power.

[Step 6: Real-time Telemetry & Ledger Audit]
Live power draw (kW) and energy delivered (kWh) are tracked on the driver's screen 
and recorded into the BOSS Charging Ledger for billing and green carbon offset credits.
