1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & SYSTEM OVERVIEW
================================================================================

1.1 PRIMARY OBJECTIVE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The primary objective of BOSS (Battery Optimized Software Service) is to build an 
intelligent, real-time, end-to-end EV charging network and smart grid load management 
platform. BOSS connects three critical actors in the EV ecosystem:
1. EV Drivers
2. Charging Station Operators (Admins)
3. Electrical Utility Distribution Companies (DISCOMs)
into a unified, real-time responsive ecosystem.

1.2 PROBLEMS ADDRESSED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. EV DRIVER CHARGING & RANGE ANXIETY:
   - EV drivers face unpredictable charging slot availability, long queue wait times, 
     lack of live port status awareness, and uncertainty regarding machine power outputs.
   - Low-battery breakdown scenarios (<10% State of Charge) lack structured emergency 
     rescue mechanisms.

2. GRID TRANSFORMER OVERLOAD & DISCOM INFRASTRUCTURE STRESS:
   - Simultaneous high-power DC fast charging (50 kW to 150 kW per vehicle) causes 
     severe localized distribution transformer overloads, phase imbalances, voltage 
     fluctuations, and grid equipment damage.
   - DISCOMs lack real-time visibility into localized EV charging demand and automated 
     tools to shave peak loads or redirect EV traffic before transformers trip.

3. COMPLETE FRONTEND FEATURE DOCUMENTARY
================================================================================

1. LANDING PAGE & CYBER-GREEN DESIGN SYSTEM (`LandingPage.tsx`):
   - High-impact visual landing experience with dark Cyber-Green theme (`#090d16`, `#00ff87`, `#60efff`).
   - Live network counters (2,800+ stations, 99.4% uptime, sub-second telemetry).
   - One-click role selector between Driver, Station Admin, and DISCOM Utility.

2. INTERACTIVE INDIA EV MAP & DISTRICT DISCOVERY (`MapView.tsx`, `FullMapPage.tsx`):
   - Leaflet map rendering charging stations across 28+ Indian States and all districts.
   - Dynamic pulsing status markers (Green for <80% available load, Red for >=80% overloaded).
   - Auto-geolocation and interactive station popups with live pricing and slot booking.

3. EXPLAINABLE AI STATION RECOMMENDATION ENGINE (`aiEngine.ts`):
   - Multi-variable AI ranking formula evaluating distance, wait time, price, and transformer load.
   - Transparent natural-language rationale explaining why each station was recommended.

4. TODAY-ONLY 1-HOUR PRE-BOOKING & 6-DIGIT SMART METER OTP (`BookSlotPage.tsx`):
   - 24 one-hour slot buckets for TODAY ONLY, preventing charger squatting.
   - Generates encrypted 6-digit Smart Energy Meter OTP code and dynamic QR payload.

5. MANDATORY CAMERA-BASED SOC & VOLTAGE OCR VERIFICATION (`SOCVerificationModal.tsx`):
   - Computer vision OCR engine that scans vehicle dashboard snapshots to detect battery percentage.
   - Cross-checks claimed vs visual SOC (±5% tolerance) to prevent queue priority manipulation.

6. AUTOMATED 80% TRANSFORMER LOAD SAFETY RULE:
   - If station load >= 80%, new reservations are locked to protect physical equipment.
   - Automatically redirects drivers to nearby underloaded stations and issues 15% discount coupons.

7. MACHINE LEARNING DC VOLTAGE & POWER TELEMETRY REGRESSORS (`aiEngine.ts`):
   - Trained linear regressors predicting required DC output voltage (V) and power (kW) based on battery SOC and charging current.

8. SOS MOBILE BATTERY RESCUE TRUCK DISPATCH (`EmergencyModal.tsx`):
   - Rapid emergency roadside assistance dispatch for low-battery (<10% SOC) or emergency vehicles.
   - Live GPS tracking, pilot dispatch telemetry, and 15-minute countdown ETA tracker.

9. LIVE USER CHARGING TELEMETRY & VOLTOPTIMIZE REWARDS (`LiveUserUsageCard.tsx`):
   - Real-time voltage, power draw, and cumulative kWh delivery tracking.
   - Calculates carbon offset (kg CO2 avoided) and green energy loyalty credits.

10. OPENROUTER MULTI-LLM AI COPILOT ASSISTANT (`AICopilotChat.tsx`):
    - Context-aware floating assistant powered by OpenRouter (Google Gemini, DeepSeek, Llama).
    - Injects real-time user state (selected station, load, booking status) to answer queries.

11. STATION ADMIN SMART VERIFICATION TERMINAL (`AdminDashboard.tsx`):
    - 6-digit OTP verification terminal to validate bookings and activate physical charger ports.
    - Live port status overrides (Available, Occupied, Fault) and transformer kVA capacity tuning.

12. DISCOM GRID OPERATOR MASTER CONTROL CENTER (`DiscomDashboard.tsx`):
    - City-wide feeder telemetry and substation transformer load heatmaps.
    - AI Event Log terminal and one-click peak-shaving off-peak incentive pushes.

13. VEHICLE PROFILE & SOH HEALTH VAULT (`VehicleDetailsPage.tsx`):
    - Vehicle database (Tata Nexon EV, MG ZS EV, Mahindra XUV400, Hyundai Ioniq 5, BYD Atto 3).
    - Battery State of Health (SOH) tracking and charging cycle analytics.

14. HISTORICAL CHARGING RECORDS & RECEIPT VAULT (`HistoryPage.tsx`):
    - Historical logs with digital green invoices and downloadable carbon reduction receipts.

15. ADVANCED GRID ANALYTICS & VISUALIZATION (`AnalyticsPage.tsx`):
    - Recharts graphs displaying 24-hour load forecasts, weekly expenditure, and connector distributions.

5. MATHEMATICAL & ALGORITHMIC FORMULAS
================================================================================
1. Haversine Distance (km) = 2 * 6371 * atan2(√a, √(1-a))
2. Station Transformer Load % = (Active Power kW / Transformer Capacity kVA) * 100
3. Explainable AI Match Score = 100 - [ w1*Dist + w2*Wait + w3*Price_Delta + w4*(Load%/10) ]
4. ML Output DC Voltage = V_nominal + (SOC% * 0.82) + (Current_Amps * 0.048)
5. Active Power (kW) = (V_DC * Current_Amps) / 1000
6. VoltOptimize Green Credits = kWh_Charged * (1 + (100 - Station_Load_%)/100)
